# Supabase email templates

Paste into Supabase → Authentication → Emails → Templates. Put the subject below into the Subject box, and the file's whole contents into Body (Source).

- `confirm-signup.html` — subject: **Confirm your email for Teamski**
- `reset-password.html` — subject: **Reset your Teamski password**
- `magic-link.html` — subject: **Your Teamski sign-in link**
- `change-email.html` — subject: **Confirm your new email for Teamski**
- `invite-user.html` — subject: **You have been invited to Teamski**
- `reauthentication.html` — subject: **Your Teamski verification code**
